I am a PhD candidate in Operations, Information, and Decisions at the Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania. My research examines how organizations monitor employee conduct and evaluate high-stakes processes when the underlying information is difficult to observe, selectively generated, or mediated by new technologies. I study these questions primarily in policing, focusing on body-worn cameras, officer discipline, racial inequality in administrative data, and AI-assisted oversight. More broadly, I am interested in organizational control, public-sector accountability, and judgment under uncertainty.
